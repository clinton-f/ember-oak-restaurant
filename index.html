Our story

Two chefs, one wood oven, zero shortcuts.

Ember & Oak began in 2019 as a food cart with a secondhand oven and a stubborn idea — that the deepest flavor in cooking still comes from fire, patience, and ingredients picked that morning.

Seven years later our dining room on Alberta Street still burns Oregon white oak every single service. No gas assist. Just the farmers we know by name and the dishes we've spent years perfecting.
